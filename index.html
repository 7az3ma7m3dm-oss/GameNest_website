SYSTEM_FAQ

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

How do I place an order?

Click PURCHASE on any pack, review the details, choose a payment method, enter your Fortnite username, upload your payment screenshot, then copy the ticket and send it to @GamenestGifts on Instagram or Discord.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 5 minutes. During peak hours it can take slightly longer.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and Telda. All payment details are listed in the Payment section above.

Do you need my Epic password?

Never. We only need your Fortnite / Epic username. Anyone asking for your password, 2FA code, or email login is not us.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled case-by-case. Message us on Instagram or Discord and we'll review your order personally.

Are V-Bucks and Crew legitimate?

Yes. All V-Bucks, Crew subscriptions, and Gifts are delivered through legitimate Epic Games channels.

Can I send a gift to a friend?

Absolutely. Just select the GIFT pack, then enter your friend's Fortnite username on the ticket instead of your own.

What if I made a mistake on my ticket?

No problem — just message the admin in the same chat before payment is confirmed. We can fix typos, change packs, or swap payment methods.
