SMALL PRINT

Questions,
answered.

Everything people ask before their first ticket.

How do I actually order?+

Pick a pack, tap Open ticket, add your Fortnite/Epic username, then use Copy Ticket, join the GameNest Discord, and create a ticket there to send it.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Only your Fortnite/Epic username goes on the ticket. The admin handles the rest of the order in chat.

Where does my ticket actually go?+

Nowhere until you send it — copying it doesn't submit anything. You paste it yourself into a ticket channel inside the GameNest Discord server.

Can I pay on the website?+

No, and that's intentional — this site never asks for payment details. Payment is arranged directly with the admin after you join the group.

Which payment methods work?+

Vodafone Cash, WE Pay, InstaPay and Telda. The admin will guide you through whichever one you use.

What if I never got a reply?+

Keep your copied ticket text — it has your order number on it — and message the admin again in the group with the same ticket.

How long does delivery take?+

It depends on how busy the counter is, but most orders are handled shortly after payment is confirmed. The admin will give you a heads-up if there's a delay.

Can I change my order after sending the ticket?+

Yes — just message the admin in the same chat before payment is confirmed. Once payment is made, changes may not be possible.

Do you offer other games besides Fortnite?+

Not yet — GameNest currently only handles Fortnite V-Bucks and Crew. If that changes, it'll be listed on this page.

Is my Epic account safe if I order?+

Yes, as long as you never share your password or 2FA code with anyone — GameNest will never ask for either. Only your username is needed to complete an order.
