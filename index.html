YOUR GUIDE TO ORDERING AT GAMENEST

How do I order?

Click PURCHASE on any pack, review the details, choose a payment method, enter your Fortnite username, then copy the ticket and send it to @GamenestGifts on Instagram or TikTok with your payment screenshot.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within a few minutes. During peak hours it may take a bit longer.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and Telda. All details are in the Payment section above.

Do you need my Epic password?

Never. We only need your Fortnite / Epic username. Anyone asking for your password is not us.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled case-by-case. Message us on Instagram or TikTok and we'll review it.

Are these official Fortnite items?

Yes. All V-Bucks, Crew subscriptions, and Gifts are delivered through legitimate Epic Games channels.
