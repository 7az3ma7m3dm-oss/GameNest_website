[FAQ // KNOWLEDGE_BASE]

SYSTEM_FAQ

How do I place an order?

Click PURCHASE on any product, fill in your name, email, phone and Epic username, upload your payment screenshot, then submit. You'll get an order code — we process and deliver as soon as payment is verified.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 5–30 minutes of payment confirmation. During high-traffic periods, it can take up to 24 hours. If we can't deliver within 24 hours, you get a full refund.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and Telda. More options coming soon.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund if: we can't deliver within 24 hours, or we send the item to the wrong account.

No refund if: you gave us a wrong Epic username, or the account was banned by Epic for reasons unrelated to our service.

How to request: message us on Instagram, Discord, or WhatsApp with your order code within 24 hours. Refunds are sent back within 3–5 business days.

How does delivery work? Are the items legit?

Yes — 100% legitimate. All items come through Epic's official Fortnite gifting system — never exploits, never stolen accounts.

V-Bucks: sent as a Fortnite Gift straight from the Item Shop to your Epic username. You need 2FA enabled and to have been friends with us for 48+ hours (Epic's rule for gifts).

Crew: purchased on the Epic Games Store and applied to your account — you get monthly V-Bucks, the Crew Pack, and the current Battle Pass.

We never ask for your password. Your account stays 100% safe.

Do you need my Epic password?

Never. We only need your Fortnite / Epic username.
