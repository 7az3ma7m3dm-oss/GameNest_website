09 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A landing page starts around 3,000 EGP. A full web application with a backend and admin panel usually starts at 15,000 EGP. We'll give you a fixed quote after the first conversation — no hourly billing, no surprises.

How long does it take?

Landing pages: 3–7 days. Full websites: 2–4 weeks. Complex apps: 4–8 weeks. We agree on a timeline up front and stick to it. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

Do you handle hosting and domains too?

Yes. We configure hosting, connect your domain, set up SSL and manage deployment. We can host on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify or a VPS — whichever fits your project best. You own the account, we just help you set it up.

Do I own the code?

Always. Everything we build is yours — the source code, the deployment, the domain. We just help you get it live and keep it running. No lock-in, no proprietary platforms, no ransom for access.

What happens after the project is delivered?

Every project includes 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, we offer optional monthly retainers for updates, monitoring and support. If you'd rather handle it yourself, we make sure the code is well documented so you can.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with clients across the Middle East and worldwide. Communication is in English or Arabic, whichever you prefer. Payments accepted via InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer or crypto.

What if I need changes during the project?

Minor changes are included. Major scope additions are quoted separately and agreed before we start them — so there are no surprise bills at the end.

Can you take over a project another developer started?

Yes — we do this often. Send us the codebase or a link to the repo and we'll audit it, tell you what's salvageable and what needs rebuilding, and give you a plan.

Do you offer refunds?

If we fail to deliver what we agreed on, you get a full refund of what's been paid. If you cancel mid-project, you pay only for the work completed up to that point.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're building. We'll reply within 24 hours with a plan, a timeline and a price.
