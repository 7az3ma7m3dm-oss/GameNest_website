Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

  • Order info: name, email, phone number, Epic username, and the payment screenshot you upload.
  • Reviews: the rating and comment you submit (plus an optional order code for the verified badge).
  • Support tickets: the name, email, optional order code, and message you send us.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order or support request.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud.
  • To display verified reviews publicly (rating + comment + first name only).

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data to third parties.
  • We never ask for your Epic Games password.
  • We don't store payment card details — payments go directly via Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, or Telda.

Where your data lives

Order, review and support data are stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore). Access is restricted to authorised staff only, protected by authentication and role-based security rules.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for up to 2 years for accounting and dispute resolution. Reviews and support tickets are kept until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on Instagram @GamenestGifts — we respond within 7 days.

Cookies & storage

We use browser local storage only to remember your cart and favorites. No tracking cookies. No ad networks. No third-party analytics.

Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out on Instagram @GamenestGifts or our Discord server.
